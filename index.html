Kitchen Organization

7 Small Kitchen Organization Ideas That Make a Big Difference

Simple ways to make your kitchen feel cleaner, more organized and more polished without a full makeover.

01

Use Clear Storage Containers

Clear containers make pantry staples easier to see while creating a more consistent and organized look.

02

Group Similar Items

Keep baking supplies, snacks, spices and everyday cooking essentials together so everything has a defined place.

03

Add a Small Countertop Tray

A simple tray can keep frequently used items together and make your countertop look more intentional.

04

Organize One Drawer

Start with your most cluttered drawer and use simple dividers or clear bins to separate everyday essentials.

05

Keep Counters Mostly Clear

Leave only the items you use regularly on the counter. Less visual clutter can make a kitchen feel instantly cleaner.

06

Coordinate Everyday Accessories

Matching or complementary colors for soap dispensers, trays and containers can create a more cohesive appearance.

07

Make Frequently Used Items Easy to Reach

Store everyday cooking tools and ingredients where you can reach them easily, while keeping less-used items tucked away.
